Who We Are

The Software Development Club (SDC) at VIT Bhopal is a student-run community for anyone who enjoys building things with code. Whether you're just starting out or already shipping side projects, there's a place for you here.

What We Do

We host workshops, hackathons, and coding contests throughout the year. Members work together on real projects, learn new tools, and help each other get better at development — from web apps to open source contributions.

Our Mission

To make learning to code less intimidating and a lot more fun, by building a community where people can ask questions, share what they know, and build cool things together.
